Project 01 • Step 03
Build Your Mood Board
Research the photos you want to make, then design a mood board for the person you choose.
Español
What a Mood Board Is
A mood board is a collage of reference images that shows the look and the feeling you are going for, before you ever pick up a camera. Photographers, designers, and filmmakers all build one. Yours collects the kinds of photos you want to make of your important person.
Study these environmental portraits. Each one shows a person in their own world, their space, their tools, their light, so you understand who they are before they say a word. That is what your Image 1 is after.
→ Scroll sideways to see all four examples.
A metalworker in his workshop
A butcher in his meat locker
A musician in his art-filled room
A football player on the field at night
Reference images are for inspiration only. They are not your photos. You will capture your own real images later with the school camera kit.
You Light Every Image With Off-Camera Flash
In Photography 2, you light all three images with off-camera flash. You will use our Godox AD200 Pro II on a light stand with a modifier, placed off to the side to shape the light the way you want. This is required for every photo.
So your research and your mood board should show the lighting look you are going for: light that is placed intentionally and shaped, not just whatever light is already there. Look for reference images with strong, directional, off-camera light.
Photographing outdoors in bright sun? You may also need ND filters to control the exposure, and a scrim with an assistant, a classmate or a family member, to soften or block the sun. Plan for that in your mood board.
Find Reference Images for Your Three People
In Step 02 you brainstormed three people. Now research all three. For each person, collect six reference images from the internet that show the kinds of photos you want to make of them.
• 2 for the environmental portrait: a person in their own space, like your Image 1.
• 2 for the first action image: a person doing the thing your person is known for.
• 2 for the second action image: that same action from a wider angle or a different moment.
That is six images per person, for all three people, so 18 reference images in all. Save them into the Inspo_Images folder inside the Important_Person project folder you downloaded on Step 01.
Look for images of your person helping or inspiring others the way they help you, doing what they are known to do. This is research, so you know what you want to capture.
DELIVERABLES · TURN IT IN
Work through every task on this page to finish this step the right way. Each step in this module gets its own grade, so turn in the work below:
• 1 inspo folder (PNG): a screen capture of your Inspo_Images folder in icon view, showing all 18 reference images, uploaded to this Canvas assignment.
• 1 mood board (JPG): your finished mood board for the person you chose, with their name in the bottom right, exported as a JPG and uploaded to this Canvas assignment.
Capture Your Inspo Folder (All 18)
Prove you did the research for all three people. Open your Inspo_Images folder and capture it.
• Set the view to icons: in Finder, click View > as Icons so every image shows as a thumbnail.
• Capture the whole screen: press Shift + Cmd + 3. A PNG of your screen saves to your Desktop.
• Check it shows all 18: your capture should show all 18 reference images.
• Upload the PNG: upload that PNG to this Canvas assignment.
Pick One Person and Make the Mood Board
Now choose. Look at your research and pick the one person with the strongest story, the one you will actually photograph. Talk to Mr. Silva about your choice. He will come around next week to see your three people and your inspiration images, so he knows who you picked and what you plan to capture.
You build one mood board, for the person you settle on. Your Inspo folder still has to show the research for all three.
• Open the template: open Important_Person_Mood_Board.psd from your project folder in Photoshop.
• Place your images: go to File > Place Embedded and add at least six of your reference images for the person you chose, two for each of your three planned images. Arrange them to fill the board.
• Add the name: in the Name: field at the bottom right, type your important person’s name.
• Save a copy as JPG: go to File > Save a Copy, change the format to JPG, and save it in your project folder.
• Upload the JPG: upload your mood board JPG to this Canvas assignment.

Qué Es un Mood Board
Un mood board es un collage de imágenes de referencia que muestra el estilo y el sentimiento que buscas, antes de tomar una cámara. Los fotógrafos, diseñadores y cineastas lo usan. El tuyo reúne el tipo de fotos que quieres hacer de tu persona importante.
Observa estos retratos ambientales. Cada uno muestra a una persona en su propio mundo, su espacio, sus herramientas, su luz, para que sepas quién es antes de que diga una palabra. Eso es lo que busca tu Imagen 1.
→ Desplázate de lado para ver los cuatro ejemplos.
Un herrero en su taller
Un carnicero en su cámara fría
Un músico en su cuarto lleno de arte
Un jugador de fútbol americano en el campo de noche
Las imágenes de referencia son solo inspiración. No son tus fotos. Captura tus propias imágenes reales después con el kit de cámara de la escuela.
Iluminas Cada Imagen con Flash Fuera de Cámara
En Fotografía 2, iluminas las tres imágenes con flash fuera de cámara. Usarás nuestro Godox AD200 Pro II en un trípode de luz con un modificador, colocado a un lado para dar forma a la luz como tú quieras. Es obligatorio para cada foto.
Por eso tu investigación y tu mood board deben mostrar el estilo de luz que buscas: luz colocada de forma intencional y con forma, no solo la que ya hay. Busca imágenes de referencia con luz fuerte, direccional y fuera de cámara.
¿Fotografiando al aire libre bajo el sol fuerte? También puedes necesitar filtros ND para controlar la exposición, y un scrim con un asistente, un compañero o un familiar, para suavizar o bloquear el sol. Plánealo en tu mood board.
Busca Imágenes de Referencia para Tus Tres Personas
En el Paso 02 pensaste en tres personas. Ahora investiga a las tres. Para cada persona, reúne seis imágenes de referencia de internet que muestren el tipo de fotos que quieres hacer de ella.
• 2 para el retrato ambiental: una persona en su propio espacio, como tu Imagen 1.
• 2 para la primera imagen de acción: una persona haciendo aquello por lo que tu persona es conocida.
• 2 para la segunda imagen de acción: esa misma acción desde un ángulo más abierto o en otro momento.
Son seis imágenes por persona, para las tres personas, así que 18 imágenes de referencia en total. Guárdalas en la carpeta Inspo_Images dentro de la carpeta del proyecto Important_Person que descargaste en el Paso 01.
Busca imágenes de tu persona ayudando o inspirando a otros como te ayuda a ti, haciendo lo que la hace conocida. Es investigación, para saber qué quieres capturar.

Elige a Una Persona y Haz el Mood Board
