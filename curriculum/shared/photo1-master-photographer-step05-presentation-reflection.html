DELIVERABLES · TURN IN 1 FILE
Work through every task on this page to finish this step the right way. Each step in this module gets its own grade, so turn in the work below:
• Your finished PowerPoint (.pptx): the full PowerPoint (cover, 3 master pages, your 3 edited photos, and the reflection closing page), presented to the class and uploaded to this Canvas assignment.
Module Resource: Your PowerPoint Template
Use this 16:9 template as your starting point. Duplicate it and make it yours. It has everything you need:
• Cover page: your master photographer’s name, your name, your period, and the course.
• 3 master pages: one master image per page, with the technique you studied.
• 3 of-your-work pages: one of your edited photos per page, with how you used what you learned.
• Reflection page: the closing page, where you answer the reflection.
All 8 PowerPoint pages: cover, three master, three of your work, and the reflection.
The template is already inside the project folder you downloaded on the Overview, in both English and Spanish.
Add Your Photos and Your Notes
Open the PowerPoint you started in Step 02 and finish it.
1Add your three edited photos. Place one of your final JPGs on each of the three My Work pages.
2Write how you used your master’s style. On each page, say what you borrowed: composition, light, the moment, tonal range, or editing.
3Fill the reflection page. Answer the three reflection questions on the closing page.
4Check every page. Make sure your name is on the cover and all six images are in place.
Share Your Work Out Loud
You will present your PowerPoint to the class. Keep it short and clear, about 3 to 5 minutes.
• Introduce your master: who they are and the style you studied.
• Walk through your three master images: name the technique on each one.
• Show your three photos: explain how each one shows your master’s influence.
• End with your reflection: what you learned and what you will carry forward.
Speak clearly and look up at your classmates. You know your work better than anyone in the room.
Finish With an Honest Reflection
Your last page is your reflection. Answer these three questions in a few honest sentences:
• What you learned: what is the most important thing you learned from your master photographer?
• What changed: how did studying their work change the way you capture or edit your own photos?
• What is next: what will you carry into your next photography project?
YOU DID IT · PROJECT COMPLETE
You studied a master, made the style your own, and presented it. That is how real photographers grow.

Agrega Tus Fotos y Tus Notas
Abre el PowerPoint que empezaste en el Paso 02 y termínalo.
1Agrega tus tres fotos editadas. Coloca uno de tus JPG finales en cada una de las tres páginas de Tu Trabajo.
2Escribe cómo usaste el estilo de tu maestro. En cada página, di qué tomaste prestado: composición, luz, el momento, el rango tonal o la edición.

Comparte Tu Trabajo en Voz Alta
Presentarás tu PowerPoint a la clase. Que sea corto y claro, como 3 a 5 minutos.
• Presenta a tu maestro: quién es y el estilo que estudiaste.
• Recorre tus tres imágenes del maestro: nombra la técnica en cada una.
• Muestra tus tres fotos: explica cómo cada una muestra la influencia de tu maestro.
• Termina con tu reflexión: qué aprendiste y qué vas a llevarte contigo.
Habla con claridad y mira a tus compañeros. Tú conoces tu trabajo mejor que nadie en el salón.
Termina con una Reflexión Honesta
Tu última página es tu reflexión. Responde estas tres preguntas en unas cuantas oraciones honestas:
• Qué aprendiste: ¿qué es lo más importante que aprendiste de tu fotógrafo maestro?
• Qué cambió: ¿cómo cambió estudiar su trabajo la forma en que capturas o editas tus propias fotos?
• Qué sigue: ¿qué vas a llevar a tu próximo proyecto de fotografía?
