Welcome to CTE Photography 2A

Photography 2 goes deeper than Photography 1. This semester you build stronger camera and lighting skills and begin learning cinematography and video. You shoot in the studio and on location, and you learn to light a scene like a pro. Take creative risks and treat each project like real work.

Fotografías en el estudio y en locación, y aprendes a iluminar una escena como un profesional. Toma riesgos creativos y trata cada proyecto como trabajo real.
