WELCOME
About This Course
Welcome to Photography 2A. You already know the basics. Now you go deeper. You will master manual exposure, sharpen your composition, work with light, and edit like a pro in Lightroom Classic. You will build a strong body of work and start thinking like a working photographer.
Manual ModeDSLR CameraEnglish & Spanish
WHAT YOU WILL LEARN
Skills You Will Build
• Master exposure: control aperture, shutter, and ISO in Manual Mode.
• Compose with intention: use the composition rules to guide the eye.
• Work with light: shape natural and studio light on purpose.
• Edit in Lightroom Classic: develop RAW files and build contact sheets.
• Build a portfolio: gather a strong, varied body of work.
• Critique and grow: give and take honest critique to level up.
HOW IT WORKS
How This Class Runs
This class is hands-on. You learn by taking photos, not just by reading. Here is what your work looks like:
Learning Modules
Short, step-by-step lessons. You build one skill at a time.
Hands-On Projects
You photograph real assignments and turn in your own photos.
Photo Walks and Critiques
We take photos together and learn from each other’s work.
Every lesson comes in English and Spanish, so you always understand the work.

CANON SETUP / STEP BY STEP
Set Up Canon Camera Connect (EOS R50)
Pair your Canon EOS R50 with your phone one time. After that you can send photos to your phone over Wi-Fi any time. Scroll through the steps.
Scroll for all the steps →
Step 1
Install the App
Download Canon Camera Connect from the App Store or Google Play on your phone.
Step 2
Turn On Wireless
On the R50, press MENU and open the wireless settings tab. Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.
Step 3
Connect to Smartphone
In the camera menu, choose ‘Connect to smartphone,’ then ‘Add a device to connect to.’
Step 4
Open the App
Open Camera Connect on your phone. Tap your camera’s name when it appears to start pairing.
Step 5
Confirm Pairing
Accept the pairing request on your phone, then confirm it on the camera. Bluetooth is now paired.
Step 6
Browse Photos
In Camera Connect, tap ‘Images on camera.’ The camera switches to Wi-Fi so you can see your photos.
Step 7
Save to Phone
Pick the photos you want and tap save. They copy to your phone.
Step 8
Upload to OneDrive
Open OneDrive and upload the photos so they reach your iPad and the lab Macs.

BIENVENIDO
Sobre Esta Clase
Bienvenido a Fotografía 2A. Ya conoces lo básico. Ahora vas más a fondo. Vas a dominar la exposición manual, afinar tu composición, trabajar con la luz y editar como un profesional en Lightroom Classic. Vas a crear un cuerpo de trabajo fuerte y a pensar como un fotógrafo de verdad.
Modo ManualCámara DSLRInglés y Español
LO QUE APRENDERÁS
Habilidades Que Vas a Desarrollar
• Dominar la exposición: controla la apertura, la velocidad y el ISO en Modo Manual.
• Componer con intención: usa las reglas de composición para guiar la mirada.
• Trabajar con la luz: dale forma a la luz natural y de estudio.
• Editar en Lightroom Classic: revela archivos RAW y arma hojas de contactos.
• Crear un portafolio: reúne un cuerpo de trabajo fuerte y variado.
• Critica y crece: da y recibe críticas honestas para subir de nivel.

Instala la App
