## REFERENCE HIERARCHY

Use the attached classroom / campus reference images only as design references for:

- architecture
- materials
- furniture design
- lighting language
- color palette
- realism level
- overall premium futuristic academic aesthetic

Do **not** copy the reference image composition, crop, camera angle, desk count, chair placement, or subject placement.

Reconstruct the room as a believable **3D architectural space** that can be viewed from any physically plausible camera angle.

If the requested view reveals areas not visible in the reference, infer them logically from the same design system.

---

## PRIMARY GOAL

Create a **hyper-realistic premium digital arts / photography classroom** that feels:

- modern
- luxurious
- exceptionally clean
- technologically advanced
- slightly futuristic
- architecturally plausible
- physically buildable
- visually calm
- professionally photographed

It should look like a **real high-end academic creative lab**, not a gaming room, cyberpunk set, spaceship, trade-show booth, or sterile CGI showroom.

---

## ARCHITECTURAL / SPATIAL LOGIC

- Use a generously scaled rectangular classroom.
- Maintain clear circulation paths and believable spacing between walls, desks, chairs, and people.
- One wall may include floor-to-ceiling glazing with dark slender mullions, but the window wall should appear only when appropriate to the requested view.
- Other walls should use clean cool-gray, silver, or soft metallic architectural panels with restrained seams.
- The ceiling should use modern paneling with long recessed white linear lights and discreet integrated fixtures.
- The room must feel coherent, measurable, and physically buildable.

Unless the scene explicitly says otherwise, the classroom has a clear **front teaching wall**, and all student workstations are oriented toward it.

---

## GEOMETRY / ALIGNMENT LOCK

Geometric correctness is a top priority.

- Keep all workstation rows aligned and intentional.
- Desks must not appear randomly rotated, crooked, warped, twisted, floating, or misaligned.
- Equivalent desks must remain consistent in size, height, thickness, construction, and leg design.
- Perspective must be coherent across the entire image.
- All walls, floor seams, desk edges, mullions, ceiling lines, and lighting lines must agree with the same camera system and vanishing points.
- Straight architectural lines must stay straight unless natural lens perspective causes convergence.
- Do not introduce AI-style warping, bowed lines, bent desk edges, or conflicting perspectives.

The camera may move freely, but the underlying room organization must remain clean, structured, and believable.

---

## DESKS / WORKSTATIONS

- Freestanding rectangular workstation desks.
- Minimal, precise construction.
- Brushed or satin silver / aluminum work surfaces.
- Realistic tabletop thickness.
- Clean square or rectangular metal legs.
- Believable structural connections and floor contact.
- No gaming-desk styling, no bulky storage pedestals, and no unnecessary cable clutter.
- Hidden or extremely discreet cable routing only.
- Subtle under-edge accent lighting is allowed when appropriate.
- Maintain a consistent furniture family across the whole room.

All desks must belong to a logical architectural grid.

---

## CHAIRS — IDENTITY LOCK

Chairs are a critical identity feature and must remain consistent.

- Modern ergonomic task chairs.
- **No armrests.**
- Lightweight open silhouette.
- Brushed or matte aluminum perimeter frame.
- Fine translucent charcoal-gray mesh back.
- Mesh must remain visibly permeable; when a person sits, their torso and clothing should still read through the mesh.
- Black or deep-charcoal upholstered seat cushion.
- Central gas-lift column.
- **Exactly five base arms and five caster positions. Never six.**
- Base must be brushed or matte aluminum, **not chrome**.
- Black dual-wheel casters with believable floor contact.

Do not let chair design drift or mutate from one workstation to another.

---

## TECHNOLOGY

When visible, use:

- premium 27-inch silver all-in-one desktop computers
- slim profiles
- silver aluminum stands
- low-profile white keyboards
- white wireless mice
- discreet cable management

Maintain consistent scale and design.

Monitors at aligned desks should face the front unless physically adjusted by a user.

Do not randomly rotate screens or distort hardware.

## MATERIALS

### Floor
- light-to-midtone sealed polished concrete
- subtle mottling and fine material variation
- mild realistic reflectivity
- believable contact shadows under furniture and people

Do not make the floor look wet, mirror-polished, or fake.

### Walls
- large flush architectural panels
- cool gray / silver / restrained metallic surfaces
- thin seams
- occasional narrow teal accent-light reveals

Keep the room visually quiet and uncluttered.

---

## LIGHTING

Use physically believable layered lighting:

### Natural daylight
- clean bright daylight when glazing is visible
- preserve exterior detail
- no blown-out white windows

### Architectural ambient light
- recessed linear ceiling fixtures
- subtle downlights
- soft wall-wash lighting

### Teal accent lighting
- primary accent color: **#007474**
- use as a restrained architectural accent only
- it may bloom slightly brighter in emission, but the identity remains #007474
- do not turn the room into a nightclub or cyberpunk environment

Lighting should produce:
- realistic global illumination
- soft falloff
- clean contact shadows
- believable reflections on metal and floor surfaces
- dimensional realism without fake HDR exaggeration

When one or more selected characters are included, they must be depicted **very accurately**.

Treat attached character references as authoritative for:

- facial identity
- skin tone
- hair
- wardrobe
- shoes
- accessories
- body proportions
- silhouette
- garment fit
- fabric behavior
- styling logic

### Character accuracy requirements
- Do **not** genericize, sanitize, normalize, or average out the character design.
- If a character has intentionally exaggerated proportions, preserve them faithfully.
- Respect the character's exact wardrobe logic, including fit, tension, drape, construction, layering, and footwear.
- Do not replace, simplify, or reinterpret key garments.
- Do not substitute generic clothing or shoes.
- Maintain accurate face likeness and body silhouette.
- Clothing must interact believably with the specific body proportions of the selected character.

If multiple characters are present, each must remain individually recognizable and proportionally consistent with their own reference.
