Create a **completely new full-body studio character-sheet image** of **[CHARACTER NAME]** in an **8:21 vertical portrait format** (portrait interpretation of 21:8). This is a **full rebuild from scratch** using the uploaded references.

- **Image A:** current approved full-body character sheet — use this as the **primary reference for body proportions, anatomy, and silhouette**.
- **Image B / C / D:** headshot references — use these for **accurate face, hair, skin tone, and identity**.
- **Image E / F / G / H / I:** outfit references — use these for **outfit appearance, garment construction, fabric logic, and fit direction only**.

## Character fidelity requirements
Depict the selected character with **strong fidelity** to the approved design.  
Preserve:
- exact approved body proportions
- approved silhouette and anatomy
- accurate face identity
- accurate head shape, hair, and facial features
- all intentional character-specific physical design choices

**Do not modify or normalize the body proportions.**  
The generator must respect the approved character exactly.  
The new wardrobe must conform to the established body correctly and realistically.

## Outfit requirements
Use the uploaded outfit images as style references only.  
Apply them to this character with attention to:

The final outfit must feel physically believable and properly constructed for the character.

## Pose and presentation
Show the character in a **3/4 rear pose** with a **natural relaxed stance**.

The **face should be looking to the side**, showing a clear **side-profile view** rather than looking back to camera.  
Expression should feel natural and composed.  
Show the **full body from head to feet**.

## Studio setup
Place the character in a **pure white seamless studio environment** with **professional model lighting**.  
The lighting should be bright, flattering, controlled, and **nearly shadowless**, with clean character-sheet presentation.  
If the eyes are visible enough, include **catchlights**.  
Render in **hyper-realistic ultra-high resolution** with a **full-frame mirrorless commercial-camera aesthetic**, sharp detail, realistic skin, realistic hair, and highly believable clothing materials.

## Important constraints
- full body only

- face turned to the side for a side-profile look

- preserve approved body proportions exactly
