ENVIRONMENT DNA

Create a premium digital arts / photography / creative technology classroom that feels:
- expansive
- luxurious
- exceptionally clean
- modern
- technologically advanced
- slightly futuristic
- architecturally plausible
- physically buildable
- visually calm
- top-of-the-line

It must look like a real high-end academic creative lab photographed with a professional full-frame camera.

Do not make it look like:
- a gaming room
- a cyberpunk set
- a spaceship
- a corporate cubicle office
- a trade-show booth
- a sterile CGI showroom

SPATIAL LOGIC

- Generously scaled rectangular classroom.
- Wide, realistic circulation paths.
- Believable clearance between desks, chairs, walls, and people.
- One exterior wall may use floor-to-ceiling glazing with slender dark metal mullions.
- Do not force the window wall into every shot.
- Other walls use clean cool-gray, silver, or soft metallic architectural panels.
- Use thin restrained panel seams.
- Ceiling uses clean modern panels with long recessed linear white lights and discreet integrated fixtures.
- Maintain long sightlines and visual breathing room.
- Furniture count and layout are determined by the current scene, not the reference image.

FLOOR

- Light-to-midtone sealed polished concrete.
- Subtle natural mottling and fine material variation.
- Mild physically plausible reflectivity.
- Soft imperfect reflections from windows, furniture, people, and accent lighting.
- Optional subtle slab seams or expansion joints when logical.
- Real contact shadows under desk legs, casters, shoes, and equipment.
- Do not make the floor look like wet glass or mirror-polished marble.

WALLS

- Large flush architectural panels.
- Cool light gray, soft silver, or restrained metallic finishes.
- Thin dark seams.
- Narrow integrated teal accent-light reveals where appropriate.
- Clean wall-wash lighting and discreet recessed downlights.
- No unnecessary posters, slogans, plaques, banners, labels, charts, logos, or invented text.
- Keep the walls visually quiet and subordinate to the people and activity.

DESKS / WORKSTATIONS

- Freestanding rectangular workstation desks.
- Minimal precise construction.
- Brushed or satin silver / aluminum work surfaces.
- Realistic metal grain and soft reflections.
- Clean square or rectangular metal legs.
- Realistic tabletop thickness.
- Structurally believable leg-to-top connections and floor contact.
- No floating tops.
- No impossible geometry.
- No bulky storage pedestals.
- No gaming-desk shapes.
- No unnecessary cable clutter.
- Cables hidden, internally routed, or extremely discreet.
- Restrained under-edge accent lighting is allowed.
- Maintain consistent furniture design across the room.

CHAIRS — HARD GEOMETRY LOCK

The chairs are a critical identity feature.

- Modern ergonomic task chair.
- Absolutely no armrests.
- Open lightweight silhouette.
- Brushed or matte aluminum perimeter frame.
- Fine translucent charcoal-gray mesh back.
- Mesh must remain visibly permeable.
- When a person is seated, their torso, waist, clothing, and body silhouette must remain perceptible through the mesh.
- Do not render the back as opaque fabric, solid plastic, or a solid black panel.
- Black or deep-charcoal upholstered seat cushion.
- Realistic central gas-lift column.
- Exactly five-point base.
- Exactly five base arms.
- Exactly five caster positions.
- Never six.
- Base is brushed or matte aluminum, not mirror chrome.
- Black dual-wheel casters with correct floor contact.
- Keep chair proportions and construction consistent throughout the room.
- Chairs may rotate naturally, but their design cannot change.

WORKSTATION TECHNOLOGY WHEN VISIBLE

- Premium 27-inch silver all-in-one desktop computers.
- Realistic slim proportions.
- Silver aluminum stand and base.
- Clean black display bezel or glass face where appropriate.
- Low-profile white keyboard.
- White wireless mouse.
- Hidden or nearly invisible cable routing.
- Maintain consistent computer scale.
- Do not duplicate or distort screens, stands, keyboards, mice, or hardware.
- Only show technology that would logically be visible from the requested viewpoint.

LIGHTING

Use a physically believable layered lighting system.

Natural daylight:
- Bright clean daylight when glazing is visible or nearby.
- Preserve exterior detail through windows.
- Avoid blown-out white windows.

Cool-white architectural ambient light:
- Long recessed linear ceiling fixtures.
- Soft wall washes.
- Restrained integrated downlights.
- Bright enough for a professional classroom without flattening all shadows.

Teal accent light:
- Core design hue: #007474.
- Use as a restrained architectural accent only.
- Natural emission bloom may appear slightly brighter or more cyan, but the underlying color identity remains #007474.
- Do not turn the room into a nightclub, gaming lab, or cyberpunk environment.

Lighting must produce:
- believable global illumination
- soft shadow falloff
- realistic contact shadows
- correct reflected light on metal
- subtle floor reflections
- realistic light interaction with mesh chair backs
- dimensionality without artificial HDR

REALISM / QUALITY

- Hyper-realistic architectural photography.
- Full-frame-camera realism.
- Natural scale and perspective.
- Correct vanishing points.
- Correct occlusion.
- Correct furniture clearance.
- Correct object-to-floor contact.
- Correct human-to-chair interaction.
- Correct desk and chair geometry at every depth.
- Straight architectural lines should remain straight when appropriate for the requested lens and camera orientation.
- Surfaces must show subtle micro-texture, realistic roughness, and believable reflections.
- Avoid plastic-looking surfaces.
- Avoid melted geometry.
- Avoid duplicate hardware.
- Avoid impossible desk legs.
- Avoid doubled casters.
- Avoid random seams.
- Avoid warped monitors.
- Avoid inconsistent furniture designs.
- Avoid the artificial over-clean AI-render look.
