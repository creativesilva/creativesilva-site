# Prompt 1 — Full Front / Neutral

Create a **completely new single full-body studio image (one clean full-body view, not a multi-panel character sheet)** of **[CHARACTER NAME]** in an **8:21 vertical portrait format** (portrait interpretation of 21:8). This is a **full rebuild from scratch** based on the uploaded references.

## Reference roles
- **Image A:** current approved full-body view for this pose (a single full-body view is ideal; the character sheet works too) — use this as the **primary source of truth** for body proportions, stance logic, limb length, silhouette, and overall character design.
- **Image B / C / D:** headshot references — use these as the **primary identity references** for the face, skin tone, facial structure, eyes, hair, expression quality, and likeness.
- **Image E / F / G / H / I:** outfit references — use these **only for outfit design, garment styling, fabric behavior, layering, construction, and fit direction**. **Do not borrow body proportions from the outfit references.**

## Character fidelity requirements
Depict the selected character with **high accuracy and full faithfulness** to the approved character sheet and headshots.  
The generator must **respect the character exactly**:
- preserve the approved **body proportions**
- preserve the approved **face and identity**
- preserve accurate **hair, facial features, and silhouette**
- do **not sanitize, normalize, reduce, or alter** intentionally exaggerated proportions
- do **not reinterpret the physique**
- the **new outfit must adapt to the character’s body**, not the other way around

## Outfit requirements
Redesign the character’s outfit using the uploaded outfit references as visual guidance only.  
Accurately follow:
- garment type
- garment layering
- fabric behavior
- seam logic
- material thickness
- stretch/compression behavior where appropriate
- drape and tension
- fit through bust, waist, hips, thighs, arms, and legs
- footwear design and construction if shoes are shown in the outfit references

The outfit should look **believable, physically wearable, and correctly fitted to this exact character body**. Preserve **fabric logic** and garment realism.

## Pose and presentation
Show the character in a **straight-on full front view**, standing in a **neutral natural stance** with a **neutral expression**.  
The pose should feel **calm, balanced, and relaxed**, not stiff or mannequin-like.  
Arms rest naturally. Weight distribution should feel realistic.  
Show the **entire full body from head to feet**.

## Studio setup
Place the character in a **pure white seamless studio backdrop** with **clean high-end professional model lighting**.  
Lighting should be bright, flattering, and controlled, with **minimal to no visible shadows**.  
Include **subtle catchlights in the eyes**.  
Render in **hyper-realistic ultra-high resolution** with a **professional full-frame mirrorless camera look**, extremely sharp detail, realistic skin, realistic textiles, and clean character-sheet presentation.

## Important constraints
- full body only
- front-facing only
- neutral natural stance
- neutral expression
- pure white background
- no props
- no scenery
- no extra people
- preserve approved body proportions exactly
- preserve face accuracy from headshots
- outfit references influence clothing only, not physique
