Cameras for the Next Generation

Donate a Camera

Put a Camera in a Kid’s Hands

My mission, and how you can be part of it

Every photographer I teach started the same way: with a camera they could hold, learn on, and make their own.

Not every student has one. A working camera you no longer use can become the tool that teaches a young person to see, to compose, to slow down, and to make something that lasts. In a world drowning in images, I am teaching students to make photographs. The right camera in the right hands is where that begins.

My mission is simple: inspire the next generation of creatives and give them the means to begin. If that mission speaks to you, donate a working camera.

What Helps

  • DSLR or mirrorless bodies, any brand
  • Compact and pocket cameras
  • 35mm film cameras in working order
  • Lenses, memory cards, and chargers
  • Tripods, bags, and clean working gear
  • Anything that still powers on and makes a frame

Gently used is perfect. If it works and can make a picture, a student can learn on it.

Support the Mission

Camera donations run through Light Writerz, the photography club and creative collective I founded at Pioneer Valley High School. Every camera goes straight into a student’s hands.
