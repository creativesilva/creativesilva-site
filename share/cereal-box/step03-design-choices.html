Español
Make the Big Choices Together
Now you and your partner make the big design choices for your box. Agree on the name, the category, the shopper, the mascot, the colors, the fonts, and the tagline.
Lock in these choices now so all six panels look like one brand, even though you each design three. Different colors and fonts on each side ruin the brand.
You fill out the Design Choices doc so you both leave with the same plan, the same HEX colors, and the same font names.
DELIVERABLES · TURN IN 1 FILE
Work through every task on this page to finish this step the right way. Each step in this module gets its own grade, so turn in the work below:
• 1 design doc: your completed Cereal Box Design Choices doc (.docx), uploaded to this Canvas assignment.
Three Steps to Finish
1Partner huddle. Talk through every choice and decide who is Designer 1 and who is Designer 2. Do not stop until you both agree.
2Fill your own doc. Every student fills out their own copy. Same answers as your partner. Same HEX codes. Same font names.
3Save and submit. Save to OneDrive, then upload the .docx to this Canvas assignment.
Name your file: CerealBoxDesignChoices_LastName_FirstName.docx.
The Six Group Decisions
• Cereal Name short, fun, easy to say. Write it exactly how it goes on the box.
• Target Audience who is it for? Write the age group and the lifestyle.
• Mascot a quick sketch or paragraph of your mascot. Give it a name.
• Color Palette pick 3 to 5 colors. Write the HEX code for each. Everyone uses the same codes.
• Three Fonts one fun title font, one accent font, one easy-to-read font for small text.
• Tagline a short phrase that sells your cereal. Goes on the front.
Every Student Turns In Their Own Doc
This is a group project, but every student turns in their own doc. Same answers as your partner, your own copy. This proves you know the plan, the HEX codes, and the fonts.

Las Seis Decisiones del Grupo
• Nombre del Cereal corto, divertido, fácil de decir. Escríbelo tal como irá en la caja.
• Público Meta ¿para quién es? Escribe la edad y el estilo de vida.
• Mascota un boceto rápido o un párrafo de tu mascota. Ponle nombre.
• Paleta de Colores elige de 3 a 5 colores. Escribe el código HEX de cada uno. Todos usan los mismos códigos.
• Tres Fuentes una fuente divertida para el título, una de acento, y una fácil de leer para texto pequeño.
• Eslogan una frase corta que vende tu cereal. Va en el frente.
