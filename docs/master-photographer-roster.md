# Master Photographer, Roster Reference

Plain-text copy of the photographer roster offered in the **Master Photographer** module
(Photography 1A, Project 02 / Fall Final Project). Students choose ONE from this roster, study
how they make their images, then capture and present three of their own photos in that style.

Source: Chris's original "Master Photographer List" Canvas page, restored into the current angular
module on 2026-11-16. The live roster lives as structured data in
`tools/build-master-photographer.py` (the `ROSTER` list); this file is the readable reference copy
so the list is never trapped in old Canvas again.

## The roster (20 photographers, 4 genres)

## Recommended research websites (general, not one per photographer)
- Artsy.net
- MoMA.org
- MagnumPhotos.com
- Tate.org.uk
- Getty.edu

Tip: for today's photographers, also search for the photographer's own official website to see
their most recent work.

## How to choose
1. Look through all four groups.
2. Pick the photographer whose work stands out to you.
3. Study their style, techniques, subjects, and editing, then use what you learn in your own photos.
